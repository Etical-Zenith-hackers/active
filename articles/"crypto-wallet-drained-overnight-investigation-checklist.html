"Crypto Wallet Drained Overnight: Investigation and Evidence Checklist

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

"What to do when a cryptocurrency wallet appears to have been drained, including account security, transaction review and evidence preservation.

# Secure the affected environment

If a wallet or connected account may be compromised, use trusted security procedures to protect remaining assets.

Secure associated email and exchange accounts as appropriate.

Review the transaction history

Identify all transactions that were not authorized.

Record each transaction hash, destination address, asset and amount.

Determine when the activity began

Build a timeline showing the first suspicious activity and later transfers.

Preserve possible attack evidence

Keep phishing messages, suspicious websites, wallet prompts, security alerts and account notifications.

Protect credentials

Never provide your seed phrase or private key to an investigator or recovery service.

Trace the blockchain

Where transactions are publicly visible, investigators can document later movements.

Report the compromise

Use official wallet, exchange, email and relevant reporting channels.

Recovery limitations

A wallet trace may establish where assets moved without guaranteeing that they can be recovered.

Final thoughts

A drained wallet should be treated as an incident requiring both security response and evidence preservation.
