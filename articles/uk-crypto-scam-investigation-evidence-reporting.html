Crypto Scam Investigation for UK Victims: Evidence and Reporting Preparation

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

An evidence-focused guide for UK cryptocurrency scam victims preparing transaction records, communications and account information for reporting.

# Document the incident

Write down what happened, when it happened, how contact began and when cryptocurrency was transferred.

Preserve transaction evidence

Keep transaction hashes, wallet addresses, blockchain networks, amounts and timestamps.

Preserve communications

Save emails, messages, websites, advertisements, usernames and payment instructions.

Keep financial records

Preserve bank statements, card records, exchange confirmations and payment receipts where relevant.

Secure compromised accounts

Use official security procedures for affected exchanges, email accounts and wallets.

Prepare a factual report

Explain what happened without making unsupported claims about identity.

Include the evidence that can be independently verified.

Consider appropriate UK channels

Depending on the circumstances, evidence may be relevant to the appropriate UK reporting, financial, legal or law-enforcement channels.

Recovery limitations

Reporting and blockchain tracing can support an investigation but do not guarantee recovery.

Final thoughts

A clear evidence package can help legitimate investigators and authorities understand a cryptocurrency fraud incident.
