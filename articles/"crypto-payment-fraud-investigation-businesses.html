"How businesses can investigate suspicious cryptocurrency payments using transaction records, invoices, wallet information and blockchain evidence.

\\ ## Cryptocurrency payment disputes

Businesses increasingly encounter cryptocurrency payments, refunds and digital-asset transactions.

When a payment is disputed or suspected to be fraudulent, documentation is essential.

Preserve invoices

Keep the original invoice, payment instructions, wallet address and amount requested.

Verify the transaction

Check the transaction hash and confirm the blockchain network and amount.

Compare payment details

A mismatch between the expected wallet address and the actual destination can be an important investigative fact.

Examine subsequent movement

Where publicly available, investigators may examine subsequent blockchain transactions.

Review internal records

Compare blockchain evidence with invoices, customer communications, order records and payment confirmations.

Protect business systems

If fraud involved compromised email or employee accounts, investigate the associated cybersecurity incident as well.

Recovery limitations

Blockchain tracing may help establish payment movement but does not guarantee recovery.

Final thoughts

A combination of accounting records, communications and blockchain evidence can help businesses investigate cryptocurrency payment incidents.
