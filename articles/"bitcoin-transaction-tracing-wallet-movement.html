"Bitcoin Transaction Tracing: Understanding Wallet Movement and Blockchain Evidence

"Learn how Bitcoin transaction records can be examined to document wallet movements and support legitimate cryptocurrency investigations.

\\ ## Bitcoin transactions are publicly observable

Bitcoin transactions are recorded on a public blockchain.

This means investigators can examine transaction histories without needing access to private wallet credentials.

Start with the transaction ID

A Bitcoin transaction ID can confirm the relevant transfer and provide information about inputs, outputs, amounts and confirmation status.

Follow subsequent movement

Where publicly visible, subsequent transactions can be examined to understand how funds moved after the original transfer.

This process is often described as transaction tracing.

Wallet addresses are not automatically identities

A Bitcoin address is not equivalent to a person's name.

Attribution may require additional information from exchanges, custodians, payment providers or other evidence.

Evidence preservation

Keep transaction IDs, wallet addresses, dates, screenshots and relevant communications.

Document where each piece of information came from.

Exchange records

If traced assets reach a regulated or identifiable service provider, investigators may be able to identify a point where additional records could exist.

Private records require appropriate authorization or legal process.

Recovery considerations

Tracing Bitcoin can provide valuable evidence without guaranteeing that funds will be recovered.

Final thoughts

Bitcoin's transparent ledger can provide a strong starting point for legitimate digital-asset investigations.
