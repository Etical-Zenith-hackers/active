How to Choose an Ethical Hacking Consultant: Questions to Ask Before Hiring

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Updated 21 October 2026

Evaluate providers by authorization, scope, methodology, evidence handling, reporting, confidentiality, and retesting criteria.

Check experience

Ask about similar systems, team roles, qualifications, and current practices. Request a redacted sample report with clear evidence, business context, and practical remediation.

Confirm scope in writing

The proposal should identify assets, environments, dates, methods, exclusions, safety controls, and emergency contacts. Changes to scope should be approved before work continues.

Review confidentiality

Discuss encryption, report distribution, storage, retention, deletion, subcontractors, and incident notification. Evidence collection should be limited to what is necessary.

Understand deliverables

Ask whether reports include methodology, impact, remediation, limitations, and an executive summary. Clarify retesting and unresolved findings; beware of guaranteed-security claims.
