Crypto Forensics Report: What a Professional Investigation Should Document

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

An overview of the information that can be included in a professional cryptocurrency forensic investigation report.

\\ ## What is a crypto forensics report?

A cryptocurrency forensic report documents relevant blockchain activity and supporting evidence in a structured format.

Executive summary

The report should explain the purpose and scope of the investigation without overstating conclusions.

Evidence sources

Document the wallet addresses, transaction hashes, screenshots, exchange records and other evidence examined.

Methodology

Explain how blockchain records were reviewed and how transaction relationships were documented.

Transaction timeline

A chronological transaction table can make complex movements easier to understand.

Findings

Findings should distinguish directly verified blockchain information from investigative interpretations.

Limitations

A professional report should clearly identify limitations.

For example, public blockchain data may show transactions without establishing the real-world identity of an address.

Recommendations

Recommendations can include evidence preservation, contacting relevant service providers or seeking appropriate legal assistance.

Recovery disclaimer

A forensic report should not promise that assets will be recovered.

Final thoughts

Clear reporting makes digital investigations easier for clients, legal representatives and authorized authorities to understand.
