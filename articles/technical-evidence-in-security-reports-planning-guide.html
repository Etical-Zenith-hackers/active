Overview

Incident readiness works best when responsibilities and decision paths are agreed before a crisis. The plan should protect people and business continuity while preserving relevant evidence.

Practical checklist

Name an incident lead and backups; keep trusted contacts available offline; identify critical systems and data; document containment options; preserve relevant logs; and prepare recovery and communications procedures.

Risks and boundaries

Do not delay urgent containment solely for evidence collection. Avoid broad, unnecessary collection of personal data, and do not assume every alert proves an intrusion or identifies an actor.

Verification and follow-up

Exercise the plan with a realistic tabletop scenario, measure time to notify and contain, test restoration, and record improvements with named owners and due dates.
