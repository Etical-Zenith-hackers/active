How to Trace Stolen Ethereum After a Wallet Draining Attack

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

Learn how stolen ETH can be documented and traced after a wallet-draining attack using Ethereum transaction records and supporting evidence.

\\## How to Trace Stolen Ethereum After a Wallet Draining Attack

Wallet-draining attacks can transfer ETH and Ethereum-based tokens from a compromised wallet.

The Ethereum blockchain preserves the transaction history needed to document those movements.

\\### Identify the Drain Transaction

Locate the transaction hash that transferred ETH or tokens away from the wallet.

\\### Review Token Transfers

A compromised wallet may lose multiple assets. Review relevant ERC-20 transfers alongside ETH transactions.

\\### Follow Receiving Addresses

Document subsequent transfers from the receiving addresses and build a chronological transaction trail.

\\### Preserve Attack Evidence

Keep malicious website addresses, messages, wallet prompts, screenshots, transaction hashes, and other relevant evidence.

\\### Secure the Remaining Wallet

If the wallet remains compromised, prioritize security before making further transactions.

Never provide private keys or seed phrases to an alleged recovery service.
