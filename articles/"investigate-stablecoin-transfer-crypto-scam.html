"How to Investigate a Crypto Scam Involving a Stablecoin Transfer

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

"An evidence-based guide to investigating suspected cryptocurrency scams involving USDT or other stablecoin transfers.

# Identify the stablecoin

Record the exact asset and blockchain network.

Stablecoins can operate across multiple networks, so network verification is essential.

Locate the transaction

Preserve the transaction hash, sending address, receiving address, amount and timestamp.

Examine the transfer path

Where publicly visible, subsequent transactions can be documented to create a transaction trail.

Preserve payment instructions

Keep the original wallet address, QR code, invoice or payment message that requested the stablecoin transfer.

Document communications

Preserve emails, messages, websites and advertisements associated with the transaction.

Consider exchange touchpoints

If assets reach an identifiable exchange or custodian, document the relevant transaction and use official reporting channels.

Avoid additional payments

Do not send more cryptocurrency simply because someone promises that an earlier payment will be released or recovered.

Recovery limitations

Stablecoin tracing can provide investigative information but does not guarantee recovery.

Final thoughts

A stablecoin investigation benefits from combining precise blockchain evidence with preserved communications and payment documentation.
