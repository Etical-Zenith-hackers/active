\\## How to Trace USDT on TRON After a Crypto Scam

TRC20 USDT transactions are recorded on the TRON blockchain. If USDT has been sent to a suspected scam wallet, the blockchain provides useful evidence for investigation.

\\### Identify the Transaction

Locate the TRON transaction hash and verify the USDT transfer.

Record the sending address, receiving address, amount, and timestamp.

\\### Follow the Token

Review subsequent USDT transfers from the destination address.

The objective is to establish a chronological trail showing where the assets moved.

\\### Preserve Off-Chain Evidence

Blockchain information should be combined with relevant messages, websites, payment instructions, emails, and other records.

\\### Understand the Limitations

A blockchain address does not automatically reveal the identity of its controller.

If funds interact with an identifiable service, additional lawful procedures may potentially become relevant.
