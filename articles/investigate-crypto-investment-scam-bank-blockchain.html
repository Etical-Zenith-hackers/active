"How to Investigate a Cryptocurrency Investment Scam With Bank and Blockchain Evidence

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

"Learn how bank records, payment instructions and blockchain transactions can be combined when investigating a suspected cryptocurrency investment scam.

# Preserve the investment communications

Save advertisements, messages, emails, website pages and investment instructions.

Document every payment

Record bank transfers, card payments and cryptocurrency transactions separately.

For crypto transactions, preserve the transaction hash and blockchain network.

Build a financial timeline

A chronological record should connect communications with payments.

Record the amount, date, payment method and destination for each event.

Compare payment instructions

Check whether the cryptocurrency address or bank details used in the actual payment match the instructions originally provided.

Preserve platform records

Keep account balances, withdrawal requests, fee demands and support correspondence.

Displayed platform balances should not automatically be treated as proof of recoverable funds.

Trace blockchain payments

Public blockchain records may help establish where cryptocurrency deposits moved after payment.

Avoid further payments

Additional requests for taxes, verification fees or release charges should be documented carefully.

Do not send additional money solely because recovery is promised.

Final thoughts

Combining banking evidence with blockchain records can provide a clearer picture of a suspected investment fraud.
