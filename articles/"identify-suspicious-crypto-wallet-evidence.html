"How to Identify a Suspicious Crypto Wallet Without Assuming Its Owner

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

"How to document suspicious blockchain wallet activity while separating verified transaction facts from assumptions about identity.

# Start with observable blockchain facts

Record the wallet address, transaction hashes, assets and transaction dates.

Examine transaction patterns

Public records may show repeated transfers, consolidation, rapid movement or interaction with service-provider addresses.

These patterns can provide investigative leads.

Avoid identity assumptions

A wallet address alone does not establish the identity of its controller.

Do not publicly accuse a person based solely on blockchain activity.

Preserve evidence

Save the relevant transaction records and document how each finding was obtained.

Compare external evidence

Communications, invoices, account records or exchange information may provide context that blockchain data alone cannot establish.

Consider service-provider information

Where a wallet appears to interact with a known exchange or custodian, the relevant transaction can be documented for lawful reporting.

Final thoughts

Responsible blockchain investigation separates confirmed transaction data from interpretations and allegations.
