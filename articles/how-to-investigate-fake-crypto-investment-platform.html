How to Investigate a Fake Crypto Investment Platform

Fake crypto investment platforms can display fabricated account balances while requesting additional cryptocurrency deposits or withdrawal fees.

If you suspect an investment platform is fraudulent, preserve the evidence.

Save the Website Evidence

Record the website address, screenshots, account balance displays, support messages, terms, payment instructions, and advertisements.

Document Every Payment

Collect transaction hashes, wallet addresses, cryptocurrency amounts, dates, and blockchain networks.

Preserve Communication

Keep emails, messages, usernames, phone numbers, and conversations with platform representatives.

Be Careful With Additional Payments

Requests for taxes, verification charges, withdrawal fees, or account activation payments should be treated cautiously.

Trace the Transactions

Blockchain analysis can help establish where cryptocurrency payments moved after being sent.
