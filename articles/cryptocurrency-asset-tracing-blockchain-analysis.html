Cryptocurrency Asset Tracing: How Blockchain Analysis Can Help Follow Digital Assets

A practical overview of lawful cryptocurrency asset tracing using wallet addresses, transaction hashes, blockchain records and relevant service-provider information.

Cryptocurrency asset tracing

Blockchain analysis can help document publicly visible transfers and organize evidence for legitimate investigations.

Evidence preservation

Preserve transaction IDs, wallet information, communications and relevant account records. Private account information should only be obtained through appropriate authorization or legal process.

Tracing is not recovery

Tracing can establish movement and investigative leads, but it does not guarantee that digital assets will be recovered.

Ethical and legal boundaries

Asset tracing should be conducted only with appropriate authorization and within applicable laws.
