Crypto Phishing Investigation: Tracing Assets After a Wallet Compromise

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

Learn how to document a cryptocurrency phishing incident, preserve evidence and investigate blockchain transactions after a wallet compromise.

\\ ## What happens during a crypto phishing incident?

Phishing attacks may trick users into revealing credentials, signing malicious transactions or connecting wallets to fraudulent websites.

Once an incident is discovered, evidence preservation is important.

Secure the remaining assets

If a wallet may still be compromised, consider moving remaining assets to a secure wallet using trusted procedures.

Do not share seed phrases with investigators or supposed recovery agents.

Identify unauthorized transactions

Record every transaction that you did not authorize.

Transaction hashes can provide an objective starting point for investigation.

Preserve the phishing evidence

Keep the original website address, emails, messages, screenshots and wallet prompts.

Do not intentionally revisit suspicious websites from an unsafe environment.

Trace the blockchain

Public blockchain records may show where assets moved after the unauthorized transaction.

This can help build a transaction timeline.

Report the incident

Depending on circumstances, consider reporting the incident to the relevant wallet provider, exchange, financial institution, cybercrime reporting service or law enforcement agency.

Recovery limitations

Tracing stolen assets does not automatically reverse transactions.

Recovery depends on the circumstances and available legal or institutional mechanisms.

Final thoughts

Fast evidence preservation and account security can be important after a wallet compromise.
