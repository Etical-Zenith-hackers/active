\\## Crypto Scam Recovery UK: Evidence Checklist for Victims

Victims of cryptocurrency scams should preserve evidence before websites, social accounts, or messages disappear.

\\### Blockchain Evidence

Collect:

\\- Transaction hashes \\- Wallet addresses \\- Blockchain networks \\- Cryptocurrency names \\- Amounts transferred \\- Transaction dates and times

\\### Communication Evidence

Preserve emails, messages, usernames, social media profiles, websites, phone numbers, invoices, and payment instructions.

\\### Financial Evidence

Keep bank statements, exchange statements, card payments, receipts, and other relevant records.

\\### Build a Timeline

Write a simple chronological explanation of what happened.

Include the first contact, promises made, payments requested, transactions completed, and when the suspected fraud was discovered.

\\### Organize the Evidence

A structured evidence file can make it easier for investigators, exchanges, solicitors, insurers, and relevant authorities to understand the case.
