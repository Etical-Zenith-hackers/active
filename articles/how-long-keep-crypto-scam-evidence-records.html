How Long Should You Keep Crypto Scam Evidence and Transaction Records?

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

Practical guidance on preserving cryptocurrency scam evidence, transaction records, communications and investigation documents for future reference.

# Keep original transaction information

Do not discard transaction hashes or wallet addresses after making a report.

Future investigative activity may depend on the original blockchain references.

Preserve communications

Keep relevant emails, messages, invoices and website information.

Keep exchange records

Save support tickets, account notifications, withdrawal confirmations and case numbers.

Maintain a timeline

Keep the chronology of events together with the underlying evidence.

Protect the evidence

Store records securely and restrict access to people who are authorized to review them.

Follow applicable retention requirements

If the evidence is connected with a business, legal case, insurance matter or regulatory process, follow the applicable retention requirements and advice of the relevant professional.

Avoid editing originals

If you need to make notes, keep them separately rather than replacing the original evidence.

Final thoughts

Cryptocurrency investigations can develop over time, so preserving the original evidence is important.
