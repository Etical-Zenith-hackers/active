How to Investigate a Stolen Bitcoin Payment Using the Blockchain

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

An evidence-based guide to investigating a stolen Bitcoin payment using transaction records, wallet addresses and publicly available blockchain information.

# Confirm the Bitcoin payment

Begin with the transaction ID associated with the suspected theft.

Confirm the sending address, receiving address, Bitcoin amount, transaction time and confirmation status.

Preserve the evidence

Save the transaction ID and wallet addresses exactly as displayed.

Also preserve exchange emails, account alerts, messages, invoices and any other evidence explaining why the payment was unauthorized.

Examine subsequent transactions

Where blockchain records are publicly visible, later transfers from the destination address can be documented.

A useful investigation records each transaction separately rather than relying on a single screenshot.

Do not assume identity

A Bitcoin address identifies a blockchain destination, not automatically a real-world person.

Attribution may require additional evidence from an exchange, custodian, payment provider or another lawful source.

Report relevant information

If the funds appear to reach a known service provider, use that provider's official reporting or compliance channel.

Relevant evidence can also be supplied to law enforcement or legal representatives where appropriate.

Recovery limitations

Blockchain analysis can document movement but does not reverse a confirmed Bitcoin transaction.

Recovery depends on circumstances outside the blockchain itself.

Final thoughts

A carefully preserved Bitcoin transaction trail can provide useful evidence for a legitimate investigation.
