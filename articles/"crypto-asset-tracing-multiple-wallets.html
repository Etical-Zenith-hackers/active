\\ ## Why multiple wallets make investigations difficult

Digital assets can move through several blockchain addresses before reaching a final destination.

This can make a transaction history difficult to understand without careful organization.

Build the transaction chain

Start with the known transaction and document each subsequent publicly observable transfer.

Record the address, transaction hash, amount and timestamp.

Separate facts from assumptions

A blockchain record can demonstrate that assets moved.

It may not establish who controlled an address.

Keep those conclusions separate.

Identify patterns

Repeated transfers, consolidations or movements into identifiable service providers may provide useful investigative leads.

These patterns should be documented rather than assumed to prove identity.

Preserve every transaction

Do not document only the first transfer.

Later transactions may become important to the investigation.

Use a chronological timeline

A timeline can show how assets moved and when significant events occurred.

Recovery considerations

Tracing multiple wallets can create useful evidence, but it does not guarantee that assets can be recovered.

Final thoughts

Complex blockchain investigations benefit from disciplined evidence collection and clear transaction mapping.
