Understand the business context

Start with the services, data, and workflows that would cause the greatest harm if unavailable or exposed. Industry-specific risks vary, so map the actual processes rather than relying on a generic checklist.

Practical checklist

Inventory critical accounts, applications, endpoints, vendors, data stores, and payment or identity workflows. Review MFA, access permissions, patching, backups, audit logs, vendor access, and incident contacts.

Prioritize realistic risks

For ecommerce, consider customer accounts, payment flows, order data, and admin access. For professional services, consider client confidentiality and document sharing. For education, consider identity lifecycle, student privacy, and shared devices. Adapt these examples to the actual environment.

Common mistakes and boundaries

Do not run intrusive tests against payment providers, SaaS vendors, or third-party infrastructure without their permission. Minimize personal-data collection and coordinate any production testing with system owners.

Verify improvements

Assign each action an owner and deadline, test backup restoration, validate access-control changes, confirm alerts work, and revisit risk after major system or supplier changes.
