How to Trace a Bitcoin Address After a Cryptocurrency Scam

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

An evidence-focused guide to examining a Bitcoin address and documenting publicly visible transactions after suspected cryptocurrency fraud.

# Find the correct Bitcoin address

Start with the transaction or payment record that connects the address to the incident.

Copy the address exactly and avoid relying on manually retyped information.

Verify the transaction

Use the transaction ID to confirm the Bitcoin amount, sending address, receiving address and confirmation status.

Examine the address history

Public blockchain records can show transactions associated with a Bitcoin address.

Relevant movements should be documented chronologically.

Follow later transfers

Where publicly observable, investigators can examine subsequent transactions to understand how funds moved after the initial payment.

Avoid identity assumptions

A Bitcoin address does not automatically reveal who controls it.

Additional attribution may require lawful records from an exchange, custodian or other service provider.

Preserve evidence

Keep transaction hashes, screenshots, messages, invoices and account records.

Report potential service-provider destinations

If the trace reaches an identifiable exchange or service, use its official reporting process.

Recovery limitations

Tracing a Bitcoin address can provide evidence but does not guarantee recovery.

Final thoughts

Accurate transaction information is the foundation of a defensible Bitcoin investigation.
