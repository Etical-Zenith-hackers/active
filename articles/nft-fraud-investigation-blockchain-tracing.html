"How blockchain evidence can support investigations involving NFT fraud, unauthorized transfers, fake marketplaces and disputed digital assets.

\\ ## NFT transactions leave blockchain records

NFT ownership and transfers may generate blockchain transactions that can be independently examined.

This can provide useful evidence when investigating suspected fraud.

Identify the collection and token

Record the relevant contract address, token ID and blockchain network.

These identifiers can distinguish the asset from unrelated transactions.

Preserve marketplace evidence

Save the original listing, seller information, transaction details, messages and payment records.

Investigate unauthorized transfers

If an NFT was transferred without authorization, record the transaction hash and destination address.

Follow the blockchain

Subsequent public transactions can sometimes be examined to document movement.

Protect accounts

Secure associated marketplace, email and wallet accounts.

Use official recovery procedures rather than attempting unauthorized access.

Recovery limitations

Tracing an NFT or its related cryptocurrency does not guarantee that the asset can be recovered.

Final thoughts

NFT investigations require both blockchain evidence and documentation from the marketplace or account involved.
