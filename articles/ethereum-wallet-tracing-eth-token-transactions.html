Ethereum Wallet Tracing: Following ETH and Token Transactions

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

An introduction to lawful Ethereum wallet tracing, including ETH transfers, token transactions, wallet addresses and blockchain evidence.

\\ ## Ethereum investigations

Ethereum supports ETH transfers as well as transactions involving many different tokens and smart contracts.

This creates a detailed public transaction history that can be examined during legitimate investigations.

Identify the correct network

Before analyzing a transaction, confirm the network involved.

Similar addresses or assets can appear across different blockchain environments, so accurate identification is important.

Transaction hashes

An Ethereum transaction hash can help establish when a transaction occurred and what addresses were involved.

Token transfers may also require examination of the relevant token contract and event records.

Wallet movement

Investigators can review subsequent transactions to document publicly visible movement.

This can help establish a chronological flow of assets.

Attribution requires additional evidence

A blockchain address alone does not necessarily identify its controller.

Attribution may require information from exchanges, custodians or other sources obtained lawfully.

Preserve evidence

Keep transaction hashes, wallet addresses, contract addresses, screenshots and communications.

Recovery limitations

Blockchain tracing can identify transaction movement but cannot independently reverse a confirmed transaction.

Recovery depends on the circumstances and appropriate legal or institutional processes.

Final thoughts

Ethereum transaction analysis can help transform complex blockchain activity into an understandable evidence trail.
