\\## How to Trace Stolen Bitcoin After an Unauthorized Transfer

When Bitcoin leaves your wallet without authorization, the blockchain provides a permanent record of the transaction.

The challenge is turning that transaction record into a clear investigative timeline.

\\### Identify the Original Transfer

Locate the transaction hash and confirm the destination address and amount.

\\### Follow the Bitcoin

Review subsequent transactions associated with the receiving address. Record relevant transfers and connected addresses.

\\### Preserve the Original Evidence

Keep wallet screenshots, exchange records, login alerts, emails, messages, and other evidence surrounding the unauthorized transaction.

\\### Separate Facts From Assumptions

Blockchain data can demonstrate that Bitcoin moved between addresses. It does not automatically prove who controlled those addresses.

Additional information may be required to establish attribution.

\\### Prepare a Case File

A structured report can organize the transaction history, supporting evidence, and relevant investigative observations.
