"Crypto Asset Recovery After Fraud: Investigation Steps and Evidence

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

"An evidence-based overview of the steps that can follow suspected cryptocurrency fraud, from transaction identification to lawful recovery processes.

\\ ## Step one: secure your accounts

If an account may be compromised, secure the associated email, exchange and wallet environment using official procedures.

Step two: identify the transaction

Find the transaction hash and verify the blockchain network.

Step three: identify the destination

Record the destination wallet address exactly as shown on the blockchain.

Step four: preserve communications

Keep messages, invoices, websites and payment instructions connected with the transaction.

Step five: trace publicly visible movement

Blockchain analysis may reveal subsequent transfers and potential service-provider destinations.

Step six: contact relevant providers

If funds reach an identifiable exchange or custodian, use its official reporting process.

Step seven: consider legal channels

Depending on the circumstances, legal representatives or law enforcement may be able to request information through appropriate procedures.

Recovery is not guaranteed

Asset tracing provides evidence. Recovery depends on many factors and should never be presented as certain.

Final thoughts

The strongest investigations begin with accurate transaction information and carefully preserved evidence.
