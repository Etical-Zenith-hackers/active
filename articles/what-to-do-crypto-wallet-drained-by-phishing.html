What to Do If a Crypto Wallet Was Drained by Phishing

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

Learn the immediate evidence and security steps to consider after a phishing attack drains cryptocurrency from a wallet.

\\## What to Do If a Crypto Wallet Was Drained by Phishing

A phishing attack can result in unauthorized cryptocurrency transfers after a victim interacts with a malicious website, signs a transaction, or exposes sensitive credentials.

\\### Secure Remaining Assets

If a wallet may still be compromised, prioritize protecting any remaining assets.

Never give a seed phrase or private key to a person claiming to provide recovery services.

\\### Record the Drain Transactions

Save every relevant transaction hash, destination address, asset, amount, and blockchain network.

\\### Preserve the Phishing Evidence

Keep the malicious website address, emails, messages, screenshots, wallet prompts, advertisements, and other evidence.

\\### Trace the Funds

Blockchain analysis may help document where the stolen assets moved after leaving the compromised wallet.

\\### Watch for Recovery Scams

Victims can be targeted by fake recovery agents promising guaranteed results.

Treat unexpected recovery offers with caution.
