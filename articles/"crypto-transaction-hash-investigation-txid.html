"Understand why transaction hashes or TXIDs are important when investigating lost, stolen or disputed cryptocurrency transactions.

\\ ## What is a transaction hash?

A transaction hash, sometimes called a TXID, is a unique identifier associated with a blockchain transaction.

It can be used to locate and verify a transaction on the relevant blockchain.

What a TXID can establish

Depending on the network, a transaction record can provide information such as:

- Sending address - Receiving address - Amount - Timestamp - Confirmation status - Transaction fees - Token or contract information

Why screenshots are not enough

Screenshots can be useful supporting evidence, but a transaction hash allows investigators to independently locate the blockchain record.

Check the correct network

Always confirm which blockchain network was used.

The same-looking address format can sometimes cause confusion across different networks.

Follow subsequent transactions

Once the correct transaction is confirmed, publicly observable subsequent movements may be analyzed.

Preserve the TXID

Store the transaction hash in more than one secure location.

Do not modify it.

Recovery limitations

A TXID can prove that a blockchain transaction occurred, but it does not reverse the transaction or guarantee recovery.

Final thoughts

For almost every crypto investigation, accurate transaction identifiers are among the most useful pieces of starting evidence.
