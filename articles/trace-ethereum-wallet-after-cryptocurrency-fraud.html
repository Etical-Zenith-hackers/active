\\## How to Trace an Ethereum Wallet After Cryptocurrency Fraud

Ethereum wallet tracing can help establish where ETH or Ethereum-based tokens moved after a suspicious transaction.

\\### Start With the Transaction Hash

Locate the original transaction and record the sending address, receiving address, amount, asset, and timestamp.

\\### Review ETH and Token Transfers

A wallet may contain ETH and multiple ERC-20 tokens. Review the relevant asset transfers rather than relying on ETH activity alone.

\\### Follow Subsequent Transactions

Document later transfers from the receiving wallet and build a chronological transaction trail.

\\### Consider Supporting Evidence

Emails, messages, websites, exchange records, and payment instructions can help establish the context behind the blockchain activity.

\\### Remember the Attribution Limit

A public wallet address does not automatically reveal the identity of its owner.
