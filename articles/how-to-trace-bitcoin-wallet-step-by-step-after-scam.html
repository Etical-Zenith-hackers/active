\\## How to Trace a Bitcoin Wallet Step by Step After a Scam

People often search for how to trace a Bitcoin wallet after cryptocurrency has been stolen or sent to a suspected scammer.

Bitcoin transactions are publicly recorded on its blockchain, allowing transaction activity to be independently examined.

\\### Step 1: Find the Transaction

Locate the Bitcoin transaction hash in your wallet or exchange account.

\\### Step 2: Confirm the Destination

Record the receiving Bitcoin address, amount, timestamp, and transaction hash.

\\### Step 3: Review Subsequent Transactions

Examine transactions involving the receiving address to establish where the Bitcoin moved afterward.

\\### Step 4: Build a Timeline

Record relevant transactions chronologically. Include transaction hashes, addresses, amounts, and timestamps.

\\### Step 5: Look for Service Connections

If the funds later interact with an identifiable exchange or service, that may provide an investigative lead.

A Bitcoin address does not automatically reveal the identity of its owner. Attribution normally requires additional evidence.
