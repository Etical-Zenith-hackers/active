Multi-Wallet Investigation

How to Investigate a Crypto Scam That Used Multiple Wallet Addresses

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

Learn how to organize a cryptocurrency fraud investigation when funds move through several blockchain wallet addresses.

# Start with the first known transaction

Identify the transaction where cryptocurrency initially left the legitimate wallet or account.

Record its transaction hash and destination.

Create a wallet map

List every relevant address discovered during the investigation.

For each address, record the transaction that connects it to the previous address.

Build a chronological chain

A transaction timeline should include:

- Transaction hash - Date and time - Sending address - Receiving address - Asset - Amount

Separate evidence from assumptions

Blockchain records can demonstrate that assets moved between addresses.

They do not automatically establish who controls each address.

Identify possible consolidation points

Multiple transfers may eventually converge on another address or service provider.

These points can provide useful investigative leads when properly documented.

Preserve all transactions

Do not stop documenting the trail after the first or second transfer.

Later transactions may become relevant.

Recovery limitations

Multi-wallet tracing can create a clearer evidence trail but does not guarantee recovery.

Final thoughts

Complex cryptocurrency cases benefit from structured transaction mapping and careful separation between verified blockchain facts and assumptions.
