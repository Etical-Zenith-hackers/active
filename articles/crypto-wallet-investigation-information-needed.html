\\ ## Starting a wallet investigation

A cryptocurrency investigation normally begins with the information already available to the asset owner.

The goal is to establish the relevant transaction and create an evidence trail.

Useful information

Depending on the case, useful information may include:

- Wallet addresses - Transaction hashes - Blockchain network - Asset type - Transaction amount - Date and time - Exchange account references - Screenshots - Invoices - Relevant communications

Never provide private keys

A legitimate investigator should not require your seed phrase or private key merely to trace a public blockchain transaction.

These credentials can provide control over assets.

Keep them confidential.

Organize your evidence

Put transaction hashes and supporting records into chronological order.

Avoid editing original files.

Explain what happened

A short factual description can be useful: what happened, when it happened, how much was transferred and where the funds were sent.

Recovery is different from investigation

An investigation may establish the movement of funds.

Recovery requires additional circumstances and appropriate legal or institutional action.

Final thoughts

Providing accurate public transaction information can help an investigator begin without compromising wallet security.
