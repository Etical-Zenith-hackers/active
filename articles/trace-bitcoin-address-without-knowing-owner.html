How to Trace a Bitcoin Address Without Knowing the Owner

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

Understand what blockchain analysis can reveal about a Bitcoin address and why additional evidence may be needed to connect an address to a real-world identity.

\\## How to Trace a Bitcoin Address Without Knowing the Owner

You do not need to know the identity of a Bitcoin wallet owner before beginning a blockchain investigation.

Bitcoin addresses can be examined through their public transaction history.

\\### What You Can See

Blockchain records may show transactions, amounts, timestamps, inputs, outputs, and other address activity.

\\### What You Cannot Assume

A Bitcoin address does not automatically reveal a person's name, physical address, telephone number, or identity.

Attribution requires additional evidence.

\\### Follow the Transaction History

Investigators can document how Bitcoin moved from one address to another and identify potential service interactions.

\\### Combine Blockchain and Off-Chain Evidence

Exchange records, communications, invoices, websites, account information, and other evidence can help provide context.
