How to Find Where Stolen Cryptocurrency Was Sent

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

Learn how blockchain transaction records can help reconstruct the movement of stolen cryptocurrency after it leaves a victim wallet.

\\## How to Find Where Stolen Cryptocurrency Was Sent

When cryptocurrency is stolen, the first question is often where the funds went.

Blockchain records can provide a starting point for answering that question.

\\### Find the Original Transaction

Locate the transaction hash from the affected wallet and confirm the destination address.

\\### Follow the Asset

Review subsequent transactions involving the receiving address.

For tokens, follow the token transfer records rather than looking only at the wallet's native cryptocurrency.

\\### Build a Transaction Map

Record important transactions in chronological order with their hashes, addresses, amounts, and timestamps.

\\### Identify Potential Service Connections

Funds may later interact with an exchange or other cryptocurrency service.

Such an interaction can provide an investigative lead, although it does not by itself prove who controls the account.
