\\## How to Trace Cryptocurrency Across Different Blockchains

Crypto investigations can become more complex when assets move from one blockchain to another.

A transaction may begin on one network and later involve another through a bridge, exchange, swap, or other infrastructure.

\\### Start With the Original Transaction

Record the original transaction hash, wallet addresses, asset, network, amount, and timestamp.

\\### Identify the Cross-Chain Movement

Determine whether the funds were bridged, swapped, deposited into a service, or otherwise moved to another blockchain.

\\### Document Each Network Separately

Each blockchain has its own transaction records. Maintain a separate record of relevant hashes and addresses before connecting the events into one timeline.

\\### Avoid Unsupported Assumptions

Similar addresses, amounts, or transaction times do not automatically prove that two wallets belong to the same person.
