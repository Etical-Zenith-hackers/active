"Cryptocurrency Fraud Evidence: Building a Case File for Investigation

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

"How to organize transaction records, communications and blockchain evidence into a structured cryptocurrency fraud investigation file.

\\ ## Why organization matters

Cryptocurrency fraud investigations can involve dozens of transactions, messages and documents.

A structured case file makes the information easier to review.

Section one: transaction evidence

Collect transaction hashes, wallet addresses, assets, amounts and dates.

Section two: communications

Preserve emails, messages, invoices, websites and payment instructions.

Section three: account records

Keep exchange confirmations, security alerts and support correspondence.

Section four: timeline

Place all known events in chronological order.

Section five: ownership

Document why the relevant wallet or assets belong to the claimant where appropriate.

Preserve original evidence

Keep original files and record where evidence came from.

Do not alter source material unnecessarily.

Protect sensitive information

Case files may contain financial and personal information.

Store them securely and limit access.

Recovery limitations

A complete case file can strengthen an investigation but does not guarantee recovery.

Final thoughts

Clear documentation helps separate verified blockchain facts from assumptions and allegations.
