"What evidence can help investigate a suspected fake cryptocurrency investment platform and trace associated blockchain transactions.

\\ ## Recognizing a suspicious investment platform

Some fraudulent platforms display fictional profits while preventing withdrawals or demanding additional fees.

When this occurs, preserve evidence before closing accounts or deleting messages.

Save platform information

Record the website address, account screenshots, deposit history, withdrawal requests and communications.

Preserve payment evidence

Keep bank transfer records, card payments, cryptocurrency transaction hashes and wallet addresses.

Examine blockchain transactions

If cryptocurrency was used, blockchain analysis may help establish where the funds were sent and whether they subsequently moved.

Document withdrawal demands

Additional demands for taxes, unlock fees, verification fees or recovery payments should be documented.

Do not send additional funds solely because someone promises that doing so will unlock previous funds.

Identify possible service providers

Investigators may identify exchanges or other services associated with the transaction flow.

Private information must be obtained through appropriate channels.

Recovery limitations

Evidence may support a fraud investigation, but no tracing service can guarantee recovery.

Final thoughts

A complete evidence package can help victims, investigators, legal representatives and authorities understand the transaction history.
