How to Prepare for a Cryptocurrency Investigation Consultation

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

Learn what information to prepare before contacting a cryptocurrency investigation or blockchain forensics professional.

\\ ## Prepare the basic facts

Write down what happened, when it happened and what cryptocurrency was involved.

Avoid assumptions about who was responsible.

Gather transaction hashes

Transaction hashes are among the most useful starting points.

Collect every relevant transaction ID.

List wallet addresses

Record the sending and receiving wallet addresses connected with the incident.

Identify exchanges

Write down any exchanges, custodians or payment providers involved.

Preserve communications

Keep relevant emails, messages, invoices and websites.

Protect your credentials

Never send seed phrases, passwords or private keys as part of an initial investigation request.

Explain your objective

The investigation may concern tracing, evidence documentation, fraud analysis or another legitimate purpose.

Clearly stating the objective can help define the scope.

Recovery expectations

A professional investigator should explain limitations rather than promise a guaranteed outcome.

Final thoughts

Good preparation can make an initial cryptocurrency investigation more organized and efficient.
