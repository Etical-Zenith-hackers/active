Crypto Recovery Solicitor Evidence: Blockchain Reports Explained

Cryptocurrency disputes can involve technical blockchain evidence that needs to be organized clearly for legal professionals.

Transaction Evidence

A report can document transaction hashes, wallet addresses, assets, amounts, timestamps, and blockchain networks.

Transaction Timelines

A chronological table can show how funds moved from an original wallet through subsequent addresses.

Supporting Documents

Relevant communications, exchange statements, invoices, screenshots, payment records, and other evidence can be referenced.

Distinguishing Facts From Conclusions

A professional report should clearly separate observable blockchain information from assumptions.

The appearance of an address in a transaction does not automatically establish its owner.

Legal Procedures

If information from an exchange or service is required, appropriate legal procedures may be necessary.
