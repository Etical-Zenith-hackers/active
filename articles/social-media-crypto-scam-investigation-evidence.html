"How to Investigate a Crypto Scam That Started on Social Media

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

"How to preserve social-media messages, payment records and blockchain evidence after a cryptocurrency scam begins through social media.

# Preserve the original profile information

Save the public profile name, username, profile URL and relevant screenshots where appropriate.

Do not attempt unauthorized access to the account.

Preserve messages

Keep conversations showing investment claims, payment instructions, wallet addresses or requests for additional funds.

Record advertisements

If the contact began through an advertisement, preserve the available advertisement details and landing-page information.

Document payments

Record bank payments and cryptocurrency transactions separately.

For blockchain payments, preserve transaction hashes and destination addresses.

Connect communications to transactions

Create a timeline showing when a payment request was made and when the corresponding payment occurred.

Watch for impersonation

Social-media profiles may impersonate legitimate people or organizations.

Document what the profile claimed rather than assuming the identity was genuine.

Recovery limitations

Blockchain tracing can provide evidence but does not guarantee that assets will be recovered.

Final thoughts

Social-media evidence can provide important context when combined with blockchain and payment records.
