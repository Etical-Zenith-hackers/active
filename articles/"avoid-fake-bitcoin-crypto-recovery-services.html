\\## How to Avoid Fake Bitcoin and Crypto Recovery Services

After losing cryptocurrency, victims may receive unsolicited messages from people claiming to have recovered or located their funds.

Some of these offers may be fraudulent.

\\### Be Wary of Guaranteed Recovery

Blockchain investigations can identify transactions and movement, but they cannot guarantee that cryptocurrency will be returned.

\\### Never Share Private Keys

Never give a seed phrase, private key, wallet password, or authentication code to an unknown person claiming to provide recovery assistance.

\\### Question Unusual Fees

Be cautious about requests for cryptocurrency payments described as activation fees, blockchain release charges, taxes, unlock fees, or recovery deposits.

\\### Verify Independently

Check professional information independently and communicate through verified contact channels.

\\### Preserve Evidence

Keep all messages and payment records from suspected recovery scammers.

These may become relevant evidence themselves.
