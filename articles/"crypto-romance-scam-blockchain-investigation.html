"Crypto Romance Scam Investigation: Following Blockchain Payment Evidence

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

"How victims of cryptocurrency romance scams can preserve communications, transaction records and blockchain evidence for legitimate investigation.

\\ ## Understanding romance-related crypto scams

Some online relationships are used to persuade victims to send cryptocurrency for supposed investments, emergencies or trading opportunities.

The emotional circumstances can make evidence preservation difficult, but documentation remains important.

Preserve conversations

Keep messages, emails, usernames, profiles, payment instructions and other relevant communications.

Do not delete the conversation after discovering the fraud.

Document cryptocurrency transfers

Record every wallet address and transaction hash connected with the payments.

Create a timeline

Document when contact began, when investment or payment requests were made and when transfers occurred.

Examine the blockchain

Blockchain analysis can help establish where cryptocurrency moved after a payment.

The blockchain may show transactions without revealing the real-world identity of the recipient.

Identify possible service-provider points

If assets move to a known exchange or other service, investigators may identify a potential point for lawful information requests.

Beware secondary scams

Victims may subsequently be contacted by fake recovery services.

Never give private keys or seed phrases to someone claiming they need them to recover funds.

Recovery is not guaranteed

Blockchain tracing can provide evidence but cannot guarantee the return of assets.

Final thoughts

Preserved communications combined with blockchain evidence can provide a stronger foundation for a legitimate investigation.
