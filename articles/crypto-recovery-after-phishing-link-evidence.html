Crypto Recovery After a Phishing Link: Evidence and Investigation Steps

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

What to document after clicking a phishing link connected to a cryptocurrency wallet, including unauthorized transactions and blockchain evidence.

# Treat the incident as a security event

If you clicked a suspicious cryptocurrency link, secure affected accounts using trusted official procedures.

Do not continue interacting with the suspicious website.

Identify unauthorized transactions

Review the wallet and record transactions that you did not authorize.

Save each transaction hash.

Preserve the phishing website

Record the website address, messages, emails and advertisements that led to the suspicious page.

Do not intentionally revisit the website from an unsafe environment.

Protect remaining assets

Follow trusted wallet and account security procedures to protect any assets that remain accessible.

Never provide seed phrases to investigators or recovery services.

Trace the blockchain

Public blockchain records may help document where unauthorized assets were transferred.

Create a chronological record of relevant movements.

Report the incident

Depending on the circumstances, notify the affected wallet provider, exchange, financial institution or appropriate authorities.

Recovery limitations

Tracing unauthorized transactions does not automatically reverse them.

Recovery depends on the circumstances and available institutional or legal mechanisms.

Final thoughts

Fast evidence preservation can help make a phishing-related crypto investigation clearer and easier to verify.
