\\## How to Recover Stolen USDT: A Practical Blockchain Investigation Guide

If your USDT has been stolen through a scam, phishing attack, unauthorized transfer, or fraudulent investment scheme, the first step is to preserve evidence.

USDT can operate across multiple blockchain networks, so identifying the correct network is important before beginning a transaction investigation.

\\### Start With the Transaction Hash

Locate the transaction hash in your wallet or exchange account. Record the sending address, receiving address, amount, token, network, and timestamp.

\\### Preserve the Supporting Evidence

Keep screenshots, emails, messages, website addresses, invoices, payment instructions, exchange records, and other information connected with the incident.

\\### Trace the Blockchain Movement

The receiving wallet can be reviewed to determine whether the USDT was subsequently transferred to other addresses or services.

Blockchain tracing can establish movement of assets, but a wallet address alone does not automatically identify its owner.

\\### Consider Lawful Recovery Channels

If the funds appear to reach an identifiable exchange or service, the transaction evidence may help support a report or lawful information request.

Recovery is not guaranteed. Results depend on the facts of the case, available evidence, movement of the assets, and applicable procedures.
