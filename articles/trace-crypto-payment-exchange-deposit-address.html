"How to Trace a Crypto Payment Sent to an Exchange Deposit Address

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

"How blockchain evidence can help document a cryptocurrency payment that appears to have reached an exchange deposit address.

# Confirm the original transaction

Locate the transaction hash and verify the blockchain network, asset and destination address.

Record the destination

Copy the exchange deposit address exactly as shown in the transaction record.

Do not assume that an address belongs to a specific person simply because it appears associated with an exchange.

Preserve exchange information

Keep deposit confirmations, support emails, account references and relevant communications.

Contact the exchange

Use the exchange's official support process if you are the legitimate account holder or authorized representative.

Provide the transaction information requested by the provider.

Blockchain tracing

If the assets later move from the destination address, publicly visible transactions may help document subsequent movement.

Private information

Exchange account ownership and customer information are generally not established solely by blockchain data.

Private records should be obtained through authorized channels.

Recovery limitations

An identifiable exchange destination may provide an investigative lead but does not guarantee recovery.

Final thoughts

The combination of transaction evidence and legitimate exchange records can provide a more complete investigation trail.
