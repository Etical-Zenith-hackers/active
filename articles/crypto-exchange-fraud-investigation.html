"Crypto Exchange Fraud Investigation: Account Records and Transaction Evidence

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

"How cryptocurrency exchange records, transaction hashes and account evidence can support a lawful crypto fraud investigation.

\\ ## Why exchange records matter

Cryptocurrency exchanges may hold information that is not available on public blockchains.

This can include account activity, transaction records and other information subject to the exchange's policies and applicable law.

Start with public blockchain evidence

Transaction hashes and wallet addresses can establish the basic movement of assets.

Preserve exchange correspondence

Save support tickets, emails, case numbers and messages from the exchange.

Document deposits and withdrawals

Create a timeline showing when cryptocurrency entered or left the relevant exchange account.

Account compromise

If unauthorized access is suspected, immediately use the exchange's official security and account-recovery procedures.

Change credentials and secure associated email accounts.

Lawful information requests

Private account information should be obtained by the account holder, authorized representative or appropriate legal process.

Do not impersonate users

Never attempt to obtain private records by pretending to be the account holder.

Recovery limitations

Exchange evidence can strengthen an investigation but does not guarantee asset recovery.

Final thoughts

Combining exchange records with blockchain evidence can create a more complete picture of a cryptocurrency incident.
