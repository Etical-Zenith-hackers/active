How to Identify a Crypto Scam Wallet From a Transaction

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

Learn how to locate and document the destination wallet associated with a suspected cryptocurrency scam payment.

\\## How to Identify a Crypto Scam Wallet From a Transaction

If cryptocurrency was sent to a suspected scammer, the destination wallet address is an important piece of evidence.

\\### Check Your Wallet History

Open the original transaction and locate its transaction hash and destination address.

\\### Confirm the Network

Identify whether the transaction occurred on Bitcoin, Ethereum, TRON, or another blockchain.

\\### Verify the Transaction

Review the transaction on the relevant blockchain explorer and confirm the amount, destination, and timestamp.

\\### Preserve the Wallet Address

Copy the complete address accurately and keep it together with the transaction hash.

\\### Continue the Investigation

The destination address can be used as a starting point for reviewing subsequent blockchain transactions and identifying potential service interactions.
