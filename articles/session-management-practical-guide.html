Overview

This guide explains session management. Start with a clear business objective and identify the assets, workflows, and people affected. The right process is proportionate to the risk and produces evidence that a decision-maker can use.

Practical checklist

Document ownership and scope; identify dependencies; use approved test accounts and synthetic data; record observations and timestamps; prioritize findings by realistic impact; assign owners and deadlines; and define how fixes will be verified.

Common mistakes and boundaries

Do not infer permission from public access, assume all vendor systems are in scope, collect unnecessary personal data, or rely on a single scanner. Pause when scope or operational impact is unclear.

Verification and follow-up

Use repeatable acceptance criteria, retest corrected controls safely, check adjacent workflows for regressions, document limitations and residual risks, and revisit the review after significant changes.
