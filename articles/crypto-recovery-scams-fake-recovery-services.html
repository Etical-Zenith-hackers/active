Crypto Recovery Scams: How to Recognize Fake Recovery Services

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

Learn how fake cryptocurrency recovery services operate at a high level and what warning signs to watch for after losing digital assets.

\\ ## A second scam can follow the first

After losing cryptocurrency, victims may search online for recovery assistance.

Unfortunately, this can attract additional fraud attempts.

Be cautious about guarantees

Blockchain investigations involve uncertainty.

Anyone guaranteeing that all lost cryptocurrency will definitely be recovered should be treated with caution.

Never share private keys

Your seed phrase and private keys can control cryptocurrency.

They should not be provided to an unknown investigator, website or recovery agent.

Watch for upfront cryptocurrency demands

Requests for cryptocurrency payments before any documented investigative work should receive careful scrutiny.

Verify identities independently

Use official business information and independently verifiable contact details.

Do not rely solely on testimonials published by the service provider.

Ask what evidence will be produced

A legitimate investigation should be able to explain its evidence-based process without requesting unauthorized access to accounts.

Preserve the original scam evidence

Keep all messages and payment requests from the original fraud as well as subsequent recovery attempts.

Final thoughts

The safest recovery investigation begins with evidence, lawful authorization and realistic expectations.
