Threat Modeling for Small Businesses: A Practical Workshop

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Updated 21 October 2026

Identify critical assets, trust boundaries, realistic threats, and prioritized controls in a focused small-business workshop.

Choose a workflow

Start with customer sign-in, invoice payment, order fulfillment, or remote access. Identify the data, services, users, and business outcomes at risk.

Map components and boundaries

Sketch applications, APIs, identity providers, databases, payment services, administrators, and third parties. Mark where data crosses organizational or privilege boundaries.

List plausible threats

Consider account takeover, fraud, unauthorized data access, disruption, misconfiguration, supplier compromise, and accidental disclosure. Use structured prompts to avoid focusing only on familiar scenarios.

Prioritize by impact

Document affected assets, preconditions, current controls, consequences, and uncertainty. Rank risks consistently and explain the business rationale; scores are decision aids, not precise predictions.

Assign and revisit actions

Give each mitigation an owner, due date, and verification method. Revisit the model after material changes or incidents.
