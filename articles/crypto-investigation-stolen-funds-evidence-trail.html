"Crypto Investigation for Stolen Funds: From Transaction Hash to Evidence Trail

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

"How an evidence-led cryptocurrency investigation can follow stolen funds from an initial transaction to later publicly visible movements.

\\ ## Start at the point of loss

Identify the transaction where cryptocurrency left the legitimate wallet or account.

Record the transaction hash, asset, amount and timestamp.

Verify the blockchain

Confirm that the transaction belongs to the correct blockchain network.

Follow subsequent transfers

Where public records allow, examine later transfers from the receiving address.

Document each step

Every transaction added to the investigation should have its own transaction hash and supporting record.

Identify possible exchange destinations

If assets appear to reach an exchange or other service, document the evidence without assuming that the service account belongs to a particular person.

Preserve supporting evidence

Communications, account alerts, invoices and screenshots can provide context around the blockchain transactions.

Separate facts from conclusions

A professional investigation should distinguish what the blockchain proves from what requires additional evidence.

Recovery limitations

Following stolen funds can produce investigative leads without guaranteeing that the funds will be returned.

Final thoughts

A careful evidence trail can make a cryptocurrency theft investigation easier to understand and pursue through appropriate channels.
