By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

"How to investigate a cryptocurrency payment made through a QR code by preserving wallet, transaction and communication evidence.

# Preserve the QR code

If a scammer provided a cryptocurrency QR code, preserve the original image or message containing it.

Do not assume that the QR code itself proves who controls the destination wallet.

Confirm the wallet address

Extract or compare the destination address with the transaction record.

Always verify the actual blockchain transaction rather than relying solely on the payment request.

Locate the transaction hash

Record the transaction hash, network, asset, amount and timestamp.

Compare the requested and actual payment

Document whether the address in the payment request matches the address used in the confirmed transaction.

A mismatch can be an important investigative detail.

Preserve communications

Keep the conversation that contained the QR code, including surrounding messages that explain what payment was requested and why.

Examine subsequent blockchain movement

Where publicly visible, later transfers can be documented.

Report relevant information

If the transaction reaches an identifiable service provider, use its official reporting process.

Recovery limitations

Blockchain tracing may establish payment movement but does not guarantee recovery.

Final thoughts

A QR-code cryptocurrency payment can still leave a useful evidence trail when the original payment request and confirmed blockchain transaction are preserved.
