"Crypto Recovery London: What Evidence Should Victims Prepare?

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

"Practical information for people in London preparing transaction records, communications and other evidence for a lawful cryptocurrency investigation.

# Start with a clear incident summary

Write down what happened in chronological order.

Include the date of the incident, cryptocurrency involved, amount transferred, destination address and the circumstances surrounding the payment.

Collect blockchain information

Useful records can include:

- Transaction hashes - Wallet addresses - Blockchain network - Asset type - Amount - Date and time - Screenshots from legitimate blockchain explorers

Preserve communications

Keep emails, messaging conversations, invoices, payment instructions and website information associated with the incident.

Do not edit original records unnecessarily.

Keep exchange evidence

If an exchange was involved, preserve account notifications, withdrawal confirmations, support tickets and case numbers.

Use the exchange's official support process for reporting.

Protect your accounts

If an account was compromised, secure the associated email, exchange and wallet environment using trusted security procedures.

Never send seed phrases or private keys to an investigator.

London and UK reporting

Depending on the circumstances, evidence may be relevant to the appropriate UK reporting, financial, legal or law-enforcement channels.

A professional investigation should explain what it can establish and what remains uncertain.

Recovery is not guaranteed

Crypto tracing may establish transaction movement without guaranteeing that assets will be recovered.

Final thoughts

Good evidence preparation can make a cryptocurrency investigation more organized and easier to verify.
