USDT and Stablecoin Tracing: Investigating Suspicious Transfers

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

How investigators can document USDT and other stablecoin transfers using transaction hashes, wallet addresses and blockchain records.

\\ ## Why stablecoin tracing matters

Stablecoins such as USDT are frequently used for legitimate payments as well as in cryptocurrency fraud cases.

Their blockchain transactions can often be examined publicly, depending on the network involved.

Identify the asset and network

USDT exists across multiple blockchain networks.

Always confirm the exact asset and network before beginning an investigation.

Document the transaction

Record the transaction hash, sending address, receiving address, amount and timestamp.

Follow the movement

Where publicly visible, subsequent transfers can be mapped to establish how the assets moved.

Investigators should distinguish confirmed blockchain facts from assumptions about who controls an address.

Centralized exchanges

Stablecoins may eventually reach exchanges or custodial services.

Those services may maintain information that is not public. Private information should only be obtained through authorized channels.

Preserve evidence

Keep transaction records, communications, invoices, wallet addresses and screenshots.

Avoid recovery guarantees

Stablecoin tracing can provide useful investigative information but does not guarantee that funds will be recovered.

Final thoughts

A properly documented stablecoin transaction trail can support legitimate fraud and asset investigations.
