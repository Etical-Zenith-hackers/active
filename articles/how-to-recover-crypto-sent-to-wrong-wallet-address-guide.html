\\## How to Recover Crypto Sent to the Wrong Wallet Address

Cryptocurrency transactions are generally designed to be irreversible, which can make payments sent to the wrong address difficult to recover.

The available options depend heavily on who controls the destination address.

\\### Save the Transaction Hash

Immediately record the transaction hash, receiving address, amount, cryptocurrency, network, and timestamp.

\\### Determine the Destination

If the address belongs to an exchange or identifiable service, contact the provider through its official support process.

If it belongs to an unknown self-custody wallet, the situation may require blockchain tracing and additional investigation.

\\### Preserve All Records

Keep wallet history, exchange records, payment instructions, screenshots, and communications.

\\### Avoid Recovery Scams

Never provide a seed phrase or private key to someone claiming to recover your cryptocurrency.

Be particularly cautious about guaranteed recovery promises and unexplained upfront cryptocurrency payments.
