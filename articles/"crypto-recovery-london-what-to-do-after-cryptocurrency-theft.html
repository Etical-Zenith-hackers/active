\\## Crypto Recovery London: What to Do After Cryptocurrency Theft

Searching for crypto recovery London services usually begins after cryptocurrency has been lost through fraud, theft, phishing, or an unauthorized transfer.

A responsible recovery investigation should begin with evidence rather than promises of guaranteed results.

\\### Preserve the Blockchain Evidence

Record every relevant transaction hash, wallet address, cryptocurrency, amount, blockchain network, and timestamp.

\\### Preserve the Incident Evidence

Keep emails, WhatsApp or Telegram messages, website addresses, social media accounts, invoices, payment instructions, and screenshots.

\\### Create a Chronological Timeline

Write down what happened from the first contact through the cryptocurrency transfer and discovery of the suspected fraud.

\\### Trace the Funds

Blockchain analysis may help establish how the cryptocurrency moved after the initial transaction.

\\### Prepare for Lawful Escalation

Depending on the circumstances, evidence may be useful when communicating with an exchange, solicitor, insurer, or relevant authority.

Crypto tracing does not guarantee recovery, but it can help turn a confusing transaction history into documented evidence.
