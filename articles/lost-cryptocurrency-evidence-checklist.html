"Lost Cryptocurrency Evidence Checklist: What to Collect Before an Investigation

By Zenith Hackers Intelligence

"A practical checklist of information to preserve when cryptocurrency is lost, stolen, misdirected or connected to suspected fraud.

\\ ## Start with the facts

Before beginning an investigation, collect everything you already know about the incident.

Avoid guessing or changing the original records.

Wallet information

Preserve relevant wallet addresses and identify which wallet belonged to you.

Never share seed phrases or private keys.

Transaction information

Collect transaction hashes, dates, amounts, assets and blockchain networks.

Account records

Keep exchange confirmations, account notifications, withdrawal records and support tickets.

Communication evidence

Preserve emails, messages, invoices, websites and payment instructions.

Identity and ownership evidence

Where appropriate, keep documents showing ownership of the account or assets.

Build a timeline

Record events chronologically.

A simple timeline can make a complex case much easier to investigate.

Preserve original files

Do not edit screenshots, emails or exported records.

Keep original versions where possible.

Recovery warnings

Be cautious of anyone demanding an upfront cryptocurrency payment while guaranteeing recovery.

Final thoughts

A well-organized evidence package can make blockchain investigations more efficient and easier to verify.
