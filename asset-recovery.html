WHAT WE CAN TRACE

Top 10 commonly lost or stolen assets

The table below shows examples of assets that may leave useful digital, registration, transaction, ownership, or tracking records. Which methods are available depends on the asset, jurisdiction, documentation, provider, and the authority available to Zenith.

What Zenith may need

  • Proof of ownership or a lawful connection to the asset.
  • Serial number, VIN, IMEI, wallet address, transaction hash, tracking number, or other available identifier.
  • Relevant dates, locations, account details, and supporting records.
  • Authorization to conduct the investigation and contact appropriate providers or authorities where required.

What “possibly recover” means

Tracing can produce a lead, identify a transaction path, locate a registered record, or establish evidence that assists a lawful recovery process. Some assets cannot be located or recovered, records may be unavailable, and third-party cooperation or legal process may be required.

No recovery result is guaranteed. Zenith should communicate findings, limitations, and next steps based on the evidence available.
